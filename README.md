## Business problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For the last two weeks, she has recorded her daily sales in a notebook and kept a rough record of her stock. She wants to know how her business is doing, whether it is growing, what she should restock, and where she should focus her effort. Using only core Python (no pandas), this project analyses 14 days of sales and a five-product stock record to answer those questions and give her practical recommendations.

## How to run

Open the notebook in Google Colab and choose **Runtime > Run all**: [![Open In Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/assets/colab-badge.svg)](https://colab.research.google.com/github/Chala-prog/-plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

No installation is needed. The notebook uses core Python only.

## Top three findings

1. **Sales are growing:** Week 2 totalled KES 24,400 against KES 22,050 in week 1, which is **10.66% week-on-week growth** (14-day total: KES 46,450).
2. **Weekends are far stronger:** Saturday and Sunday average KES 4,900 a day against KES 2,685 on weekdays, which is **82.5% higher**. The best day was Saturday of week 2 (KES 5,600).
3. **Two products need restocking now:** Tomatoes (18 kg vs reorder level 25 kg) and Sukuma (9 kg vs 15 kg). Avocado has the best gross margin at **42.9%**, while Onions have the lowest at **30.0%**.
